What we do

Procurement and supply chain management
Sourcing, purchasing, distribution and warehousing of goods, equipment, tools, materials, machinery and industrial products, managed end to end for corporate and government buyers.
General contracts and construction
Construction services, maintenance, renovations and alterations, delivered as contracts we plan, execute and manage.
Oil and gas support services
Engineering support, technical services, industrial supplies, manpower support and facility support for operators and service companies, including multinational oil corporations.
Import, export and trading
Importation, exportation, sales and distribution of general goods. We also act as commission agents and manufacturers’ representatives.
Logistics, haulage and transportation
Road transportation, carriage and haulage, goods carriers and equipment leasing. Air and sea transport are not part of our services.
Business and facility services
Administrative and business support, outsourcing, facility maintenance and cleaning, and operational support for client sites.
Agro-allied products and farming
Agro-allied products and farming, including arable, poultry, livestock, mechanised and organic farming.

A new company built for dependable delivery

Grandcrest Integrated Services Ltd was incorporated in Nigeria in September 2026 as a private company limited by shares. We bring procurement, contracting, logistics and support services under one roof, so a client can brief one company instead of coordinating several.

We are based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, and serve clients who need materials, equipment and technical support delivered on schedule and documented properly.
